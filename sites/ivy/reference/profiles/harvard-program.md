Harvard has had two head coaches in program history. Kathy Delaney-Smith led the Crimson for 39 seasons, winning 621 games, 11 Ivy League regular-season titles and six NCAA Tournament berths. Carrie Moore succeeded her and is in her sixth season, with a 93-55 record entering it.

In 2025-26 Harvard reached the Ivy League Tournament final as the third seed, beating second-seeded Columbia 67-65 in the semifinal before losing the title game to Princeton, 63-53, and then won two WBIT games. Home is Lavietes Pavilion.
