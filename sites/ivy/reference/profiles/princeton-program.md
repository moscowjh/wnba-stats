Lauren Gosselin is in her first season as Princeton's head coach, promoted after six seasons as an assistant. She succeeded Carla Berube, who left for Northwestern after winning the Kathy Delaney-Smith Mid-Major Coach of the Year award for 2025-26.

That season Princeton won the Ivy League Tournament, beating Harvard 63-53 in the final, and finished 26-4. The Tigers reached the NCAA second round in 2015, 2022 and 2023. Kaitlyn Chen, class of 2024, was drafted into the WNBA in 2025 and plays for Golden State. Home is Jadwin Gymnasium.
