Megan Griffith returns for her 11th season behind the bench for Columbia, where she played for the Lions herself. She is by far the most successful coach in program history, with a 171-99 record entering this season.

Last season ended with a championship, just not the one Columbia came for. Seeded second at the Ivy League Tournament, the Lions lost the semifinal to Harvard 67-65, then won five straight in the WBIT, beating Wisconsin 67-50 and BYU 81-64 in the final. Home is Levien Gymnasium.
